Factzchecker — Multi-Layer Fake News Detection & Analysis

  • Python
  • Flask
  • DistilBERT
  • NLP
  • Web Verification
  • 8B-Parameter LLM

Built a web application that classifies news content as real or fake using a three-layer verification pipeline instead of relying on a single model.

  • Layer 1 – NLP detection: Fine-tuned a DistilBERT model on 60,000 real and fake news articles to detect fake news from the text itself.
  • Layer 2 – Live fact-checking: Fetched information from the internet to cross-check the claims in the article and judge whether they are true or fake.
  • Layer 3 – LLM judge: Used an 8B-parameter language model as the final judge, weighing the classifier output and the web evidence to give the final verdict.
  • Earlier version: TF-IDF features with a LinearSVC classifier, with saved model/vectorizer artifacts.
  • Planned features: User news submission, history, dashboards, and analytics.
